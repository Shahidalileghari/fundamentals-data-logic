# 02 — Logic and Boolean algebra

Propositions are true or false. NOT reverses truth; AND requires both true; OR requires at least one true; XOR is true when values differ. De Morgan: NOT(A AND B) = NOT A OR NOT B; NOT(A OR B) = NOT A AND NOT B. Short-circuiting may skip evaluation of the second operand. Practice: access is allowed iff authenticated AND (owner OR administrator); construct its truth table.
