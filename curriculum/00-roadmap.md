# Roadmap

20 lessons: 01 Computational thinking; 02 Logic and Boolean algebra; 03 Algorithms and pseudocode; 04 Problem analysis and edge cases; 05 Programming language/runtime model; 06 Variables, types and expressions; 07 Control flow; 08 Functions, scope and recursion; 09 Binary and data representation; 10 Memory, references and collections; 11 Complexity analysis; 12 Arrays, strings and hashing; 13 Linked lists, stacks and queues; 14 Trees and heaps; 15 Graphs and traversal; 16 Searching and sorting; 17 Debugging and errors; 18 Testing and correctness; 19 Modularity and design; 20 Engineering workflow and capstone.

For each lesson: read, trace examples manually, implement from memory, test normal/boundary/invalid inputs, analyze complexity, and explain the trade-offs. Completion means independently solving a new problem and defending correctness.
